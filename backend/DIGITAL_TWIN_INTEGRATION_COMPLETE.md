## Key Features Enabled

### 1. **Live Vehicle State Tracking**
- Real-time RPM, Speed, Fuel Level, Temperature, Battery Voltage
- GPS coordinates (lat/lng) when available
- Vehicle status: PARKED, IDLING, MOVING, OFFLINE
- Ignition status and engine hours

### 2. **Telemetry Source Awareness**
- `SIMULATED` - Generated by telemetrySimulator.js (default)
- `REAL` - From Android OBD app or MQTT devices
- Auto-fallback: REAL → SIMULATED after 60 seconds of no data

### 3. **Realistic Simulation Engine**
- **4 Driving Modes:**
  - PARKED (30% probability) - RPM=0, Speed=0
  - IDLING (20%) - RPM 720-880, Speed=0
  - CITY (35%) - RPM 1200-2500, Speed 15-60 km/h
  - HIGHWAY (15%) - RPM 1800-3200, Speed 60-100 km/h
- **Smart Physics:**
  - RPM leads speed changes (acceleration curve)
  - Coolant temp warmup over time (30→95°C)
  - Fuel consumption per hour
  - Mode changes every 40-160 seconds
  - Real odometer advancement per kilometer

### 4. **Driver Behavior Detection** (Auto)
- HARSH_ACCEL - Δ RPM > 1500 in 2 sec
- HARSH_BRAKE - Δ Speed > 20 km/h deceleration
- IDLE - RPM > 0 for > 30 sec without moving

### 5. **Alert Engine** (Auto)
- LOW_FUEL - < 15% fuel
- LOW_BATTERY - < 12V
- OVERHEAT - > 95°C coolant
- OFFLINE - No update > 30 sec

### 6. **Dashboard KPIs** (Real-time)
- Online vehicles (last update < 30s)
- Currently moving vehicles
- Fleet utilization %
- Average fuel level across fleet
- Average RPM

### 7. **Socket.IO Broadcasting**
- `live:update` emitted every 2 seconds per vehicle
- Sent to rooms: `vehicle:{id}` and `user:{userId}`
- Real-time dashboard updates without polling
